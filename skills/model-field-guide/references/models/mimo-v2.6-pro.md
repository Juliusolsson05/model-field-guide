# MiMo-V2.6-Pro — today's top MIT open model

**Feel:** A launch-day surprise from Xiaomi: MIT weights at the top of the open leaderboard. Promising, unproven.

**Shines:**
- Tops AA's Intelligence Index among open-weights models at launch, tied with Grok 4.7 (independent, via VentureBeat).
- MIT license — the most permissive terms at this tier.
- Omnimodal: text, image, video and audio in; 1M context.
- Unusually open release: technical report, RL environments and training framework published alongside the weights.

**Falls over:**
- Most agent-benchmark numbers are Xiaomi's own runs.
- Claude Opus 5 still leads it on several code and cyber evals (VentureBeat).
- 1.02T total parameters (42B active) — heavy to self-host.
- Launch-day: no hands-on reviews yet.

**Reach for it when:** you need open weights with MIT terms and frontier-adjacent capability; research on RL/agent training.

**Avoid when:** production agentic work before independent evaluations land.

**Effort:** UNVERIFIED — reasoning controls not confirmed in sources read.
