# Grok 4.7 — the cautious runner-up with live X data

**Feel:** Reliably second place on knowledge work, cheap per token, and more willing to say "I don't know" than most.

**Shines:**
- Knowledge-work evals: second only to Fable 5.1 on GDPval-AA and AA-Briefcase at launch (AA via Decrypt).
- Lower hallucination rate than Grok 4.6 on AA's hallucination benchmark — restraint, not higher accuracy (AA).
- Live X/Twitter search; low per-token price for its tier.
- Available on day one in Cursor and the xAI API.

**Falls over:**
- Pricier per task than its per-token price suggests on CursorBench — it spends more tokens (Decrypt).
- Multimodal still behind rivals; xAI's own leadership said so.
- Slow time to first token (secondary reports).
- Shipped after repeated delays; very little hands-on evidence yet.

**Reach for it when:** real-time or X-sourced information; knowledge-work tasks where hallucination risk matters more than peak quality; a cheap worker alongside a Claude/GPT lead.

**Avoid when:** interactive coding where latency matters; vision-heavy work; top-end coding (Opus 5.5 / Sol are better value).

**Effort:** `low`…`xhigh`. AA's headline figures are at `xhigh`; `high` is a separate row.
