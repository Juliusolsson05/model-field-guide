# GPT-6 Astra — the computer-user

**Feel:** Ambitious and relentless: give it a screen and a goal and it keeps working for hours.

**Shines:**
- Computer use: operating complicated desktop and web apps for long stretches; Zvi relays reports that computer use is essentially solved (independent reviewers, Every and Zvi).
- 3D, Blender, games and visual one-shot builds.
- Writing: Every called it the best writing model they had tried — fast, easy to steer.
- Coordinating subagents on large "ambitious" projects; frugal with tokens per task.

**Falls over:**
- Stops at arbitrary points or, conversely, runs on without a time limit; hard to predict which.
- Overcomplicates, and has a recognizable generic "slop" look in design output (Every rated it yellow overall).
- Weaker product instincts than Fable 5.1 (Every).
- Headline ARC-AGI-3 results depend heavily on the harness — don't compare across labs.

**Reach for it when:** browser/desktop automation, frontend QA by actually clicking through the app, long-running app-driven tasks, writing that needs little editing.

**Avoid when:** you need product judgment or the simplest design (Fable 5.1); cost-sensitive work (premium price); you can't bound its runtime.

**Effort:** `low`…`max`. Vendor computer-use numbers are usually at high effort; name the effort when you compare.
